The Full Circle, in the bubble

Same circle, new home. After Devcon 8 in Mumbai, the circle came home to Chiang Mai — to the 4Seas community center: free coworking, open to the public 24 hours a day, with the Zuzalu Library next door. Here it floats in a bubble under the palms, and two Naga keep watch, like the serpents on the temple stairs of Lanna.

Inside is the Rider Sanctuary: the house piano and electric drum kit, and the U·BASS and ukulele that Kala gave — the ones that travelled the whole road. Every one of them plays.
