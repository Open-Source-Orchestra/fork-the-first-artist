Steward desk · the circle's chain of trust

Invite an artist. Approve their claim.

Nobody claims a name in the circle alone. A steward invites, the artist consents with their own wallet, a steward approves — and only then does the ops key issue <name>.osoartist.eth to the artist's own address. Every step is a free signature; no gas, no funds move here.

  1. Root · owner of osoartist.eth
  2. Stewards · people the root trusts (grants)
  3. Invitation · a steward invites one artist
  4. Consent · the artist signs with their own key
  5. Approval · a steward checks it and signs
  6. Name · issued to the artist's wallet; OSO keeps no record rights
